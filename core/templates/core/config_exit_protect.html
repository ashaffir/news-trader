Descriptions
  • Dynamic exit: Close the position when signed_change_% over the last dynamic_exit_window_minutes is below −dynamic_exit_drawdown_tolerance_pct. The sign is aligned to the trade direction (negative means adverse move).
  • Profit protect: When a favorable move is detected — signed_change_% over the last profit_protect_window_minutes is at least profit_protect_threshold_pct — tighten the trailing stop to profit_protect_trailing_distance_pct.
  • Formulas:
    signed_change_% = 100 × (p_now − p_Nmin_ago) / p_Nmin_ago (auto‑signed by direction)
    Long trailing SL: SL = highest_since_open × (1 − profit_protect_trailing_distance_pct / 100)
    Short trailing SL: SL = lowest_since_open × (1 + profit_protect_trailing_distance_pct / 100)
